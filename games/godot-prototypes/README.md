# Godot Prototypes

Small Godot 4 experiments — early movement and scene-setup work, kept as a record of
things I tried rather than as finished games. The completed project from this line of
work is [`learn-to-fly`](../learn-to-fly).

| Project | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cold-project` | The most developed of the three — player and character-body controllers, a game scene, and parallax cloud and map art |
| `cold-protocol` | Minimal player-movement scene |
| `new-game-project` | Movement scratchpad |

Open any of these by pointing the Godot editor at its `project.godot`.
