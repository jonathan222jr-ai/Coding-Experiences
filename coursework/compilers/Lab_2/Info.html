About Merchants and where to find them

This page contains information about Merchants and where to find them.

Merchants serve as key NPCs who provide maps, tools, upgrades, and information throughout Pharloom. Each has a distinct personality and role, from Shakra the wandering vendor to Forge Daughter in the Deep Docks. Some merchants are hidden or require quests to unlock their services. They help guide progression by offering items that strengthen Hornet or expand her mobility. Their presence makes the world feel alive and interconnected

List of the Merchants:

  • Shakra - Wandering merchant, first met in The Marrow
  • Pebb - Merchant in Bone Bottom
  • Forge Daughter - Merchant in Deep Docks
  • Grindle Merchant in The Marrow (freed from prison)
  • Mottled Skarr - Merchant in Hunter's March
  • Jubilana - Wandering merchant in Songclave/Choral Chambers
