Topic: Silksong(Groups Members: Anthony Ramirez, Jonathan Ramirez, and Henry Valladares )

Silksong is the highly anticipated sequel to Hollow Knight, following Hornet as she journeys through the mysterious kingdom of Pharloom. The game expands on exploration with new biomes, enemies, and faster combat mechanics. Hornet's agility and silk-based abilities give the gameplay a sharper, more aggressive feel. Players uncover secrets, battle challenging bosses, and navigate a world filled with traps and treasures. Like its predecessor, Silksong blends atmosphere, lore, and precision platforming into a deep experience.
