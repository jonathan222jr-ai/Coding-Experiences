List of Tools throughout Silksong

Here we will talk about some of the tools and their locations in silksong

Tools are special items Hornet can equip to enhance combat, defense, or exploration. They are divided into categories such as Red (offensive), Blue (support/defense), and Yellow (exploration/utility). Some tools unleash direct attacks, while others provide buffs, healing, or resource generation. Players collect them through vendors, hidden chests, quests, or boss drops. Building the right combination of tools allows unique strategies for different encounters.

List of the Tools:

    Red Tools
  • Straight Pin - Starting tool, given early
  • Threefold Pin -Found in a chest, early area
  • Sting Shard - Dropped by minor enemy
  • Tacks - Bought from Shakra
  • Longpin - Hidden chest, mid-game zone
  • Curvesickle - Found in mossy caverns
  • Throwing Ring - Vendor purchase
  • Pimpillo - Reward from NPC sidequest
  • Conchcutter - Found in Coral Forest
  • Silkshot - Crafted with materials
  • Delver's Drill - Dropped by Delver miniboss
  • Cogwork Wheel - Chest in Greymoor
  • Cogfly - Reward from arena fight
  • Rosary Cannon - Purchased from relic dealer
  • Voltvessels - Found in electric caverns
  • Flintslate - Hidden cache, deep area
  • Flea Brew - Given by Flea NPC
  • Plasmium Phial - Quest reward, Elder Hu
